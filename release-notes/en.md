# Release notes

What changes for you in each version of the app. The newest version is first.

## Unreleased

No change.

## 0.1.17 - 2026-10-31

- **What's new** shows bold text in bold, no longer between asterisks.

## 0.1.16 - 2026-10-31

- A garment can have the **reference** of the product in the shop.
- Type a **colour code** as `#1a2b3c`, `rgb(…)` or `hsl(…)`: the garment takes the nearest colour of the palette.
No change.

## 0.1.15 - 2026-10-31

- With an account, your garments now **sync between your devices**. Changes made offline stay saved and go out when the connection comes back. Settings shows the time of the last sync.

## 0.1.14 - 2026-10-31

- The buy date of a new garment starts at today.
- A garment can now have a **buy date** and a **price**, both optional. Its detail also shows when you added it to the app.
- The **brand** field suggests known brands as you type, your own brands first.
- **Random outfit** in Wardrobe suggests an outfit of your garments in the colours of a combination. Tap **Another** to see a different one.
- **Delete all data** in Settings removes the garments and the preferences from this device, and the app starts as new.

## 0.1.13 - 2026-10-30

- On a computer or a tablet, the app uses the wide screen: a side menu with the name of the app, **Add garment** at the top, the detail of a garment in a card beside the list, and Settings in two columns.

## 0.1.12 - 2026-10-27

- The app has a new icon: a sweater on a hanger with colour swatches.

## 0.1.11 - 2026-10-25

- You can use the app without an account: tap **Use offline** on Sign in. Your clothes stay on this device. Sign in with Google later from Settings.

## 0.1.10 - 2026-10-25

- You sign in with your Google account. The administrator approves each new account; your garments stay on the device.
- Each garment keeps its size, how it fits and its measures, such as the waist or the leg. A new garment gets the measures of the garments of its kind.

## 0.1.9 - 2026-10-24

- The title of the app is in the language of the app, for example in the window and the list of open apps.

## 0.1.8 - 2026-10-24

- `Settings` › `Help` › `What's new` lists what changes in each version. After an update, it opens by itself once.
