# Release notes

What changes for you in each version of the app. The newest version is first.

## Unreleased

No change.

## 0.1.26 - 2026-11-01

- The **Monochrome** and **Analogous** palettes no longer hold two nearly equal colours in one combination.
No change.

## 0.1.25 - 2026-11-01

- The **Photos** / **Colour** switch of the cards leaves **Wardrobe** and moves to **Settings**, under **Preferences**.

## 0.1.24 - 2026-11-01

- New group **Matching** in **Settings**, with four optional rules over the Sanzo Wada combinations. All of them start off.
- **Complete with neutrals** adds neutral shoes to each outfit, and under **Cold** a coat too.
- **Contrast** puts the outfits of high or soft contrast first.
- **Colour type** (spring, summer, autumn or winter) keeps in **Fill** only the colours that suit you.
- **Formality** adds a level to each garment and removes outfits that mix levels far apart, such as a suit with trainers.
- **Palettes** now has seven colour families: Sanzo Wada, complementary, analogous, triadic, split complementary, neutrals and accent, and monochrome. Pick one at the top of **Palettes** or in **Settings**; it gives the outfits of the whole app, **Wardrobe** and **Fill** too.
- In **Settings**, **Palette families** lets you turn off the families you do not want.

## 0.1.23 - 2026-11-01

- New **Fill** tab: it suggests the garments that would give you more outfits, both basics and Sanzo Wada colours that complete palettes. Each colour says which palettes it completes. Tap one to add it with its type, its season and its colour already set. You can hide it in **Settings**.
- **Missing basics** leaves **Wardrobe**: the basics are now in **Fill**, next to the Sanzo Wada colours.

## 0.1.22 - 2026-11-01

- The brand field shows its suggestions again as you type: a list of brands under the field. Tap one to use it.

## 0.1.21 - 2026-11-01

- A garment with no photo shows a drawing of its type (T-shirt, shirt, jumper, trousers, shorts, skirt, jacket, coat, shoe, boot, cap, scarf, belt or bag) in its colour. The type comes from the name of the garment.
- In **Wardrobe** you can choose **Photos** or **Colour**: with **Colour** each garment shows its colour in place of its photo.
- The app has a new icon: three lengths of fabric in Sanzo Wada colours, to match the new design.
- **Missing basics** suggests the basic garments that you do not have yet, such as jeans or a white T-shirt. The ones that give you more outfits come first. Tap one to add it with its type, its season and its colour already set.
- The **introduction** is new: it explains the outfits, **Palettes** and **Missing basics**, says that the app works online and offline, and no longer names the slide to delete or the + button.

## 0.1.20 - 2026-11-01

- On a phone, **Add garment** is a wide button under the title, and there is no floating + button. So **Settings** no longer has the **Hand** option.
- On a phone, a bar at the top shows **ATTIRE**, and the bottom bar shows the sections by name only.
- **Settings** explains **Account** and **Modules**, shows how many garments **Export** writes, and **Delete all data** has its arrow.
- The season and colour filters are smaller. In **Random outfit** and the **Palettes** detail, the season is beside the title. **Wearable only** is no longer in a card.
- The app no longer uses green: **Exact** is grey, the switches are black and **Fit** is text. **Delete** is underlined.
- The pictures of an outfit fill their place, with square corners.
- From a tablet, the app takes a centred frame, as the **Blanco de catálogo** proposal shows. The **Wardrobe** summary says of how many Sanzo Wada combinations your outfits come.
- **Add garment** no longer has a + sign, and the detail of a garment does not repeat the title of the section.
- The **season filter** is three separate buttons, and the pressed one is black. In **Random outfit** it is no longer cramped.
- The **outfits of a detail** show how many there are beside their title.
- A long name of one word no longer runs out of its column.
- In the detail of a garment with several images, the large photo slides to the next one (or with the arrows from a tablet), and the colour band says which one it is, as "2 / 3". The row of thumbnails is gone.

## 0.1.19 - 2026-11-01

- The **main button** is black.
- From a tablet, the sections are in a **top bar**, and each section shows a summary under its title.
- To delete a garment, open it and tap **Delete**. A row no longer slides.
- **Wardrobe** is a grid of photos grouped by slot. Each garment shows its colour and how many outfits it has. With no photo, it shows its colour.
- The **detail of a garment** shows its photo large with a band of its colour, and the outfits show the photo of each garment.
- **Palettes** shows each combination as a bar of its colours, and its detail shows the colours large with their name and code.
- **Settings** groups each option in a card, and **Install the app** is a button.
- With a screen reader, each garment of the grid is read once.

## 0.1.18 - 2026-10-31

- The **Back** of the phone, also the slide from the edge of the screen, closes the detail of a garment or of a combination. It no longer closes the app.

## 0.1.17 - 2026-10-31

- **What's new** shows bold text in bold, no longer between asterisks.

## 0.1.16 - 2026-10-31

- A garment can have the **reference** of the product in the shop.
- Type a **colour code** as `#1a2b3c`, `rgb(…)` or `hsl(…)`: the garment takes the nearest colour of the palette.
No change.

## 0.1.15 - 2026-10-31

- With an account, your garments now **sync between your devices**. Changes made offline stay saved and go out when the connection comes back. Settings shows the time of the last sync.

## 0.1.14 - 2026-10-31

- The buy date of a new garment starts at today.
- A garment can now have a **buy date** and a **price**, both optional. Its detail also shows when you added it to the app.
- The **brand** field suggests known brands as you type, your own brands first.
- **Random outfit** in Wardrobe suggests an outfit of your garments in the colours of a combination. Tap **Another** to see a different one.
- **Delete all data** in Settings removes the garments and the preferences from this device, and the app starts as new.

## 0.1.13 - 2026-10-30

- On a computer or a tablet, the app uses the wide screen: a side menu with the name of the app, **Add garment** at the top, the detail of a garment in a card beside the list, and Settings in two columns.

## 0.1.12 - 2026-10-27

- The app has a new icon: a sweater on a hanger with colour swatches.

## 0.1.11 - 2026-10-25

- You can use the app without an account: tap **Use offline** on Sign in. Your clothes stay on this device. Sign in with Google later from Settings.

## 0.1.10 - 2026-10-25

- You sign in with your Google account. The administrator approves each new account; your garments stay on the device.
- Each garment keeps its size, how it fits and its measures, such as the waist or the leg. A new garment gets the measures of the garments of its kind.

## 0.1.9 - 2026-10-24

- The title of the app is in the language of the app, for example in the window and the list of open apps.

## 0.1.8 - 2026-10-24

- `Settings` › `Help` › `What's new` lists what changes in each version. After an update, it opens by itself once.
