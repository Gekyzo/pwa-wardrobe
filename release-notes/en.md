# Release notes

What changes for you in each version of the app. The newest version is first.

## Unreleased

No change.

## 0.1.10 - 2026-10-25

- You sign in with your Google account. The administrator approves each new account; your garments stay on the device.
- Each garment keeps its size, how it fits and its measures, such as the waist or the leg. A new garment gets the measures of the garments of its kind.

## 0.1.9 - 2026-10-24

- The title of the app is in the language of the app, for example in the window and the list of open apps.

## 0.1.8 - 2026-10-24

- `Settings` › `Help` › `What's new` lists what changes in each version. After an update, it opens by itself once.
